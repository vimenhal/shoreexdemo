Itinerary

Day by day

Angkor is one of the most important archaeological sites in Southeast Asia. Stretching over some 154 square miles, Angkor Archaeological Park contains the magnificent remains of the different capitals of the Khmer Empire from the 9th to the 15th century.

  1. 1

    Leave the ship and head to Wat Lue — a renowned hilltop Buddhist temple.

    Then, transfer to the airport for a flight to Siem Reap. Check in at the hotel and enjoy lunch in the hotel restaurant.

    This evening, a dinner of Khmer food is served at deluxe local restaurant.

    Hotel:Sokha Hotel (or similar)
  2. 2

    After breakfast at the hotel, you’ll drive to Banteay Srei to visit the Citadel of Women. This petite temple possesses some of the most intricate carvings in the Angkor area. Although it is small, the details and quality of its architecture make it a must-see attraction.

    Your next visit the magnificent temple at Angkor Wat — a unique and stunning blend of spirituality and symmetry and an enduring example of human devotion to religion. It is the largest and undoubtedly the most breathtaking of the monuments of Angkor, and it is reputedly the largest religious structure in the world.

    After lunch, you will visit the Angkor Thom complex to see the Terrace of Elephants, the Leper King and the Bayon. Explore the Bayon Temple, whose giant stone faces have become one of the most recognizable images connected to classic Khmer art and architecture. Continue to Ta Phrom Temple, famous for its forest atmosphere and enormous fig trees, looks just as it did when the first European explorers arrived. It is also the location of many scenes filmed for the blockbuster movie Tomb Raider.

    You will drive back to town for some time to explore the Old Market or shop for a simple memento of your stay in Cambodia.

    Back at the hotel, there’s time to freshen up before dinner with a traditional Apsara dance show.

    Hotel:Sokha Hotel (or similar)
  3. 3

    Enjoy an early breakfast at the hotel; then check out and transfer to the airport for your flight to Ho Chi Minh City, where you will reboard the ship.
